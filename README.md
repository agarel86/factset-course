# FactSet in Excel

Course material for Audencia Business School (SFIR): retrieving and analysing FactSet data in Excel with the FactSet 365 add-in.

- `course/`: the course pages (English, French in `course/fr/`) and the solution workbooks of the tests (`course/excel/`).
- `covid project/`: a short research project on French listed firms during the COVID-19 crash, with its workbook and datasets.

The workbooks need a FactSet account and the FactSet 365 add-in to refresh their data.
