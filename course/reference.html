Module 8

Troubleshooting

Based on FactSet's official page FactSet 365 Troubleshooting Steps (OA 22644).

What you seeCauseWhat to do
#NAME? in every FactSet cell, and Refresh changes nothingThe file was opened before the FactSet 365 add-in had finished loading, so Excel did not recognise FDS. Refresh skips cells that are already in error.1. Check that the FactSet 365 tab is there and that you are signed in (Refresh is not grey).
2. Select the sheet (Ctrl+A⌘ Cmd+A), open Find & Replace (Ctrl+HCtrl+H), replace = with =, click Replace All. Every formula is re-entered and shows #Calc.
3. FactSet 365 › Refresh › Workbook.
#NAME? in one cellFDS typed in lower case, or the add-in is not loaded.Retype FDS in capitals. Reload the add-in: Home › Add-ins › FactSet 365 (under My Add-ins).
Refresh is grey, or every cell stays on #CalcYou are not signed in to FactSet 365.FactSet 365 › Sign In, then Refresh › Workbook. Large files take a minute: wait until no #Calc remains.
"Add-in Error" when opening a fileThe file was saved with another add-in instance.Click Restart in the notification, or click Insert Formula.
"Page Expired" in Excel on the webSafari.Use Google Chrome.
FactSet 365 cannot be added on a PCAn older FactSet desktop add-in is installed (some university PCs).Use Excel on the web in Chrome, or ask IT.
#SPILL!A series has no empty cells to spill into.Clear the cells below or to the right.
NA or empty resultWrong identifier, wrong code, no data at that date.Check the identifier in the search bar, rebuild the formula with Insert Formula, try another date, look at the same data on the FactSet online platform.
Wrong date returnedDate typed DD/MM/YYYY.FactSet dates are MM/DD/YYYY.
Formula refusedSeparator of your Excel language.Use ; between the Excel arguments if your Excel is not in English.
Values differ from the FactSet online platformCurrency, unit, date or fiscal period differ.Check the arguments in Insert Formula (period ANN/QTR, date, currency).

Still stuck: FactSet's live chat and help (? icon at the top right of the FactSet online platform; Office Assistance in the FactSet tab), or the support contacts on the Key resources page.

Glossary

IdentifierCode of a security: ticker-region (MSFT-US), ISIN, CUSIP, SEDOL, or a FactSet entity identifier (007XXT-E).
ISIN12-character international security identifier (country code + national code + check digit), e.g. FR0014004628.
CUSIP / SEDOLNational identifiers: CUSIP (US, 9 characters), SEDOL (UK, 7 characters; designates one listing).
ReportA page of the FactSet online platform about one company or security (Snapshot, Price History…).
ScreenA query that returns the list of securities or deals meeting criteria (Universal Screening, Screen On).
Insert FormulaFactSet 365 panel that searches items and builds the =FDS formula.
Item / code / sourceThe variable (Price), its code (P_PRICE) and the database it comes from (Prices).
SpillA formula returning several values that fill the cells below or to the right.
#CalcA FactSet formula waiting for FactSet 365 › Refresh.
ReturnPrice change over a period: P(t) / P(t−1) − 1.
Fiscal yearThe 12-month accounting period of a firm; it may not end in December (Microsoft: June).
FY1 / FY2First / second fiscal year not yet reported.
EPSEarnings per share.
ConsensusAggregate (mean, median) of analysts' forecasts.
BrokerBank or research firm whose analyst publishes an estimate.
EBIT / EBITDAOperating income / operating income before depreciation and amortisation.
CapexCapital expenditure: investment in fixed assets.
Market capitalisationShare price × shares outstanding.
P/EShare price / earnings per share.
Holdings / weightThe securities a fund owns / the share of each in the fund's value (%).
Report dateDate of the filing that gives a position.
ETFExchange-traded fund; one that replicates an index holds its members (IVV-US, CAC-FR, QQQ-US).
%OSPercentage of shares outstanding held by a shareholder.
Institutional holderFund manager, bank or insurer that files its positions (families and companies are not included).
Herfindahl indexSum of squared shares (weights in %: 0 to 10,000); measures concentration.
BetaSlope of a stock's returns on market returns.
CorrelationCo-movement of two series, from −1 to 1.
Basis point0.01 percentage point; changes in yields are measured in basis points.
Coupon / coupon at issuanceAnnual interest rate paid by a bond / the rate set when it was issued.
Maturity / years to maturityDate the bond is repaid / time left until then.
Green bondBond whose proceeds finance environmental projects.
Country of domicile / of riskWhere the issuer is legally based (its nationality) / where its business is exposed.
Deal value (disclosed)Price of an M&A deal; often not disclosed, which is not 0.
Long format panelOne row per unit × period.
Survivorship biasBias from keeping only companies that still exist today.
