Key resources & official documentation

Where FactSet documents its data, its formulas and the Excel add-in. Use these pages when you need the full definition of a data item, the complete list of arguments of a formula, or help on a tool.

Log in firstThe links below open FactSet's Online Assistant (OA) and FactSet files. They require you to be logged in to my.apps.factset.com with your own account. Each OA page has a number (e.g. "OA 1967"): you can also type that number in the Online Assistant search box.

1. The Online Assistant

The Online Assistant is FactSet's official documentation. From the FactSet online platform, click the ? icon (top right); from Excel, click Office Assistance in the FactSet tab.

  • Online Assistant home: search box (full site or page number), Get Started, Database Help, Functions Library, Tipsheets, Training, Contact Support.
  • Getting Started with FactSet (OA 15728) and Navigating FactSet tipsheet (PDF)
  • Data Products (OA 8): description of every database (prices, fundamentals, estimates, ownership, deals, ESG, economics…).
  • Downloading FactSet Tipsheets (OA 11330): one-page PDF guides for each application.
  • Training & eLearning (OA 12304): seminars and videos.
  • Contact Support (OA 4129): phone numbers and support email.

2. FactSet 365 (the Excel add-in)

  • Getting FactSet 365 (OA 23024): installation in Excel on the web, Excel for Windows and Excel for Mac.
  • Using FactSet 365 (OA 21538): sign in, Insert Formula, identifiers, refresh.
  • FactSet 365 Troubleshooting Steps (OA 22644): #NAME errors, add-in errors, Safari issues, limits.
  • Office Integration (OA 20038): all FactSet tools in Excel, Word and PowerPoint.
  • Sidebar in Excel (OA 16664) and the Sidebar eLearning course.

3. Formula syntax

  • =FDS Formulas Quick Reference (PDF): the most used price, fundamental and estimate formulas on two pages. Print it.
  • =FDS Codes (OA 1967): what =FDS codes are, syntax, examples.
  • Definitions of Code Arguments (OA 1964): items, dates (MM/DD/YYYY, relative dates, fiscal periods), frequencies, currencies.
  • Using Arrays with =FDS Codes (OA 2166): time series in Excel.
  • Cell Referencing (OA 509): building codes from cells.
  • Categorical List of Functions (OA 1411): date, statistical, lookup and array functions usable inside codes.
