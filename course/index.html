Module 0

Collecting financial data with FactSet and Excel

At the end of this course you will be able to find a company, a fund, a bond or a transaction on FactSet, understand the data shown, and bring it into Excel, either with formulas that refresh or by downloading tables, to build a research sample.

How the course works

  1. Part I, the FactSet online platform (Modules 1–2). You log in to FactSet online and learn to navigate it. Then we go through every type of data available, using Microsoft as the example.
  2. Part II, Excel (Modules 3–4). You install the FactSet 365 add-in and learn how FactSet formulas work.
  3. Part III, building samples (Modules 5–7). You learn a method, complete 14 tests, then collect the data for your own thesis. You try each test on your own first; its solution comes with an Excel file whose formulas are already written.

Follow the modules in order. Excel files are given to you at the step where you use them.

Module 1

FactSet and your access

FactSet is a provider of financial data. It collects, cleans and links market prices, company accounts, analyst forecasts, shareholders, bonds, funds, transactions, news and economic statistics for companies all over the world.

What you have access to

ToolWhat it isWhat you use it for
The FactSet online platform
my.apps.factset.com		FactSet in your web browser. It is a navigator: you search a company, a security, a fund, an index or a person, and you open its pages (reports). You also use apps such as screening tools, news and charts.Finding a company or security, reading its data, building lists with the screening tools, downloading tables to Excel.
FactSet 365
(add-in for Excel)		A FactSet tab inside Excel and a worksheet function, =FDS(), that asks FactSet for a data item and writes the answer in the cell.Collecting data for many companies, variables or dates with formulas you can refresh and reuse.

Both use the same personal account: the email address and password you set up from the FactSet email you received. You do not have the FactSet desktop application (Workstation), and you do not need it: everything in this course is done via the FactSet online platform or the FactSet 365 add-in for Excel.

1.1 First login

  1. Open the email you received from FactSet and click the activation link. Choose your password. Your username is your institutional email address.
  2. Go to https://my.apps.factset.com in Google Chrome (recommended by FactSet). Enter your email address, then your password. You may be asked for a verification code.
  3. You land on the FactSet home page. In this course, screenshots come from our institution's real access: you will see exactly the same screens.
Your account is personalNever share your password and never use someone else's account. Every query is logged under your name.

1.2 The screen after login

When you log in, no company is open yet. You see the black top bar and a default page with the day's news (Today's Top News).

The top bar is always there, whatever page you open:

  • Search FactSet (left): the box where you type a company, a ticker, a fund, an index or a person. This is how you start almost everything (section 1.3).
  • Tabs that open the main apps: Today's Top News, Watchlist, Markets, Portfolio Monitor, Company/Security, Document Search, Charting, News.
  • My Apps (right): the list of every application in our access (section 1.5).

To see data about a company, you first have to find it with the search box.

1.3 Finding a company: an example with Apple

Click in Search FactSet and type Apple. Do not press Enter yet.

  • Each result shows the name, the identifier and the type: Apple Inc. · AAPL-US (NAS) · Telecommunications Equipment. Other results are not the company you want: Apple Hospitality REIT (another company), "Apple (ZCE CNY/mt) Continuous" (an apple futures contract), a person (Oliver Schusser, Apple employee), a PE/VC firm.
  • Under each result, shortcuts open a report directly: Snapshot (@SNP), Price History (@PH), All Estimates (@EST).
  • The right panel previews the company: price, CUSIP, first trading date, volume, beta, next earnings date, average analyst rating and target price.
  • ID Lookup (bottom left) opens an advanced search by identifier, country, exchange or type of security.

Press Enter or click Apple Inc.: Apple's Snapshot opens.

You are now in the Company/Security page of Apple. It has three zones:

  1. The identifier box (top left, under the black bar): it shows the company you are looking at, here AAPL-US. Type another identifier there (e.g. MSFT-US) to switch company while staying on the same report.
  2. The Reports menu (left column): every report available for the company, grouped into folders (Overviews, Charts, News, Research and Filings, Prices, Ownership, Financials, Estimates, Credit Analysis, Transactions…). Section 1.4 lists them all.
  3. The report (centre): the page you selected, here the Snapshot (summary). At its top right: Currency (LOCAL by default), the download arrow ↓ (section 1.6) and other tools.

Identifiers

AAPL-US is the FactSet identifier: ticker + - + country of the listing. LVMH on Euronext Paris is MC-FR, Microsoft is MSFT-US. The other identifiers of the share are in the Key Stats panel of the Snapshot: CUSIP, SEDOL, ISIN (for Microsoft: US5949181045). You will use these identifiers in Excel formulas in Module 4.

1.4 The Reports menu

Open Microsoft: type MSFT in Search FactSet and press Enter. The Reports menu on the left lists every report available for the company, grouped into folders. Below is the same menu as in our access: click any report to see in detail what you will find in it.

Reports marked ▣ have a screenshot in Module 2. The menu adapts to the type of security: a fund (e.g. IVV-US) shows Fund Overview, Fund Flows and a Holdings folder instead of Financials (section 2.13).

1.6 Downloading a table to Excel

Almost every report and every panel has a download arrow ↓, at the top right of the report or of the panel. Click it and choose Excel. The file contains the table as values, not formulas. You will use this in the tests to download lists (bonds, deals, holders) and paste them into the course workbooks.
